Ask the people who set these rules

These rules are set by state legislatures and utility commissions, so that's where a request counts.

What you can do

  • Check your state's rule so you know what to ask for: a new rule, a better trigger, or wider coverage.
  • Find your state legislators and tell them what your state's rule does, or that it has none.
  • Look up when your state utility commission takes public comments on shut-off rules.
  • If you are facing a shut-off, call your utility and ask about payment plans and assistance. This site is not legal advice, and rules change.
